What happens when an HVAC call is missed

  1. A homeowner calls because the AC quit on a Saturday or the heat is out on a cold night. Your techs are on other calls and the phone rings out.

  2. Under a minute later, their phone buzzes: a text that says you got the call and asks what is happening at the house.

  3. Their reply lands in one inbox, flagged urgent if the house has no heat or no cooling at all, so dispatch calls back the right job first.

Sample moment

Sample: Saturday, 2:40 PM in July. The AC quits in a house with two kids home for the summer. The owner is up on a commercial roof finishing a repair across town and misses the call. The customer gets a text in under a minute, replies “no cool air at all, house is at 86,” and the lead lands in the owner's inbox flagged “Emergency — no cooling.” He calls back from the truck, puts the job on the board for that evening, and the customer stops calling down the list.

Questions HVAC owners ask

What does a customer get when I miss their call?

A text back in under a minute, day or night. It says you got their call and asks what they need. Their reply lands in one inbox, and urgent jobs are flagged first.

We have a dispatcher. Is this still useful?

Only for the hours nobody is in the office, and for the stretches when every line is already tied up — the first hot week, the first cold snap, and the Monday after a storm. Nights, weekends, and lunch count too. If every call already gets picked up right away, you do not need this.

Does it answer my phone live?

No. It follows up by text after a call is missed. If you want a voice to pick up the call itself, see the AI Phone Receptionist from Northbeam Builds.

What does it cost an HVAC company, in total?

$199 to set it up, then $99 a month. The first month is $298 total. Month-to-month, no long contract, and no per-message bill.

How long does setup take?

You fill in a short form, about five minutes, with your hours, services, and where leads should go. We build it, you test it on your own phone, then it goes live. Text replies need a carrier registration that takes 2–5 business days. The email path goes live right away.
