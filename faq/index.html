How fast is “instant”?

Under a minute, day or night.

What do I have to do?

One short call. We collect your hours, services, and where leads should go. We build it, you test it on your own phone, then it goes live.

What does the first month cost, in total?

$298 total: the $199 one-time setup plus $99 for the first month of service. After that it's $99/month, month-to-month.

What if the customer replies with a hard question?

The reply starts the conversation and captures the details. Anything real gets flagged to you right away — you take it from there.

Does it answer my phone live?

No. Never-Miss-a-Lead follows up after a missed call or message, by text. If you want a voice to pick up the call itself, see the AI Phone Receptionist from Northbeam Builds.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's month-to-month. Cancel anytime and you owe nothing further.
